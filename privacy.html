Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

DailyAllowance is built so that your financial records stay yours. The developer does not run servers for the app, does not collect your data, and cannot see your records.

What the app stores

Your balances, entries, bills, savings and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is turned on, they are also stored in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can access. The developer has no access to it.

No tracking or analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Network requests

  • Exchange rates. If you use more than one currency, the app downloads public exchange rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). Requests contain only currency codes and dates, never amounts, names, notes or identifiers. Like any web server, Frankfurter receives your IP address.
  • Apple services. Maps and place search, iCloud sync, Sign in with Apple and purchases are provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: to scan a receipt you choose. Text recognition, including Apple Intelligence where available, runs on your device. Images and recognized text are not saved or uploaded.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to fill in an entry from your words. Recognition runs on your device. Audio is not saved or uploaded.
  • Location (only while using the app): to suggest a place for a new entry. It is saved with the entry only if you keep it, and you can remove it at any time. The app never uses background location.
  • Motion: to animate reflections on the allowance display. Motion data is not saved.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. The app requests no name or email address. It receives only an anonymous identifier from Apple and stores it in your device's Keychain to remember that you signed in. It is not sent anywhere else.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. The app receives only the transaction information that Apple provides to confirm your access. It never sees your payment details.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries at any time, or erase everything from Settings in the app. Deleting the app removes the data on your device. To remove iCloud data, open Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage on your iPhone. To stop using Sign in with Apple with this app, open Settings > [your name] > Sign in with Apple.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
