Support

Need help or found a problem? Open an issue and describe what happened. Please don't include amounts, account names or other personal financial details.

How is today's allowance calculated?

The app takes the money in the accounts you include, sets aside unpaid bills until your next payday and the savings you protect, and divides what is left by the days remaining, including today. Spending less raises later days; spending more lowers them.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: the daily allowance, logging expenses and income, history, backup and export, up to 2 accounts and up to 5 recurring bills in your home currency.

Pro adds widgets, receipt scanning and voice entry, insights and the spending map, unlimited accounts and recurring payments, savings goals and foreign currencies. If Pro ends, nothing you created is locked or deleted.

Free trial and subscriptions

The monthly and yearly plans include a one-week free trial for new subscribers. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To manage or cancel, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase.

Restore purchases

On a new device, sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app's Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

Back up and move your data

With iCloud turned on, your records sync through your private iCloud account. You can also export a backup file from Settings > Backup and import it on another device.
